Real storms. Game rules.

NASA observed the solar eruptions used in this game. We saved these records on 15 November 2026. This is an archive, not a live warning service.

Dates, speeds, and angular widths come from NASA DONKI. The Mars encounters and arrival times are WSA–ENLIL model predictions, not measurements of radiation at our base.

The shield is imaginary. Real crews use shielding materials and storm shelters. Power, Air, and Crew are game units, not medical measurements. Days are compressed into seconds; angular width becomes game-wave duration. Those are design choices.

In this game the air machine needs a reserve of 20 power. The shield spends that same battery. At zero power, release the shield before raising it again.
